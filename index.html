About

I'm a design student and founder based in Toronto, currently studying at Seneca Polytechnic @ York University. I think about design the way an editor thinks about a sequence: what comes first, what lingers, what gets cut.

That instinct started with video editing, which I'm still learning, and it's quietly shaped how I approach brand and product work too: pacing, rhythm, and knowing which frame says the most.

Currently building Critiqu, an AI design critique platform. Open to freelance work and collaborations.
